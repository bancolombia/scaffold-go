Templates

Templates are embedded from internal/template/templates and executed by TemplateEngine. Data structs live in internal/template/models.go.

Project (init)​

TemplateOutput
project/go.mod.tmplgo.mod
project/main.go.tmplcmd/main.go
project/config.go.tmplconfig/config.go
project/config.yaml.tmplconfig/config.yaml
project/README.md.tmplREADME.md
project/Makefile.tmplMakefile

Domain​

TemplateOutput
domain/entity/entity.go.tmplinternal/domain/{name}.go
domain/entity/repository.go.tmplinternal/domain/{name}_repository.go
domain/entity/entity_test.go.tmplinternal/domain/{name}_test.go
domain/interface/interface.go.tmplinternal/domain/{lowercase-name}.go (I{Name})
domain/usecase/usecase.go.tmplinternal/usecase/{name}_usecase.go
domain/usecase/usecase_test.go.tmpltests
domain/entity/publisher.go.tmplDomain-side publisher port (when used)

HTTP entry-points​

TemplateOutput
ep/http/handler/http_handler.go.tmpl{name}_handler.go
ep/http/handler/health_handler.go.tmplhealth
ep/http/handler/module.go.tmplFx module
ep/http/routes/*.tmplrouters
ep/http/middleware/cors.go.tmplCORS
ep/http/server.go.tmplFiber server
ep/http/handler_test.go.tmpltests

Consumers and publishers​

Under ep/consumer/{rabbitmq,ibmmq}/ and da/publisher/{rabbitmq,ibmmq}/, plus infrastructure/messaging/{rabbitmq,ibmmq}/module.go.tmpl.

Driven adapters​

AreaTemplates
Database{postgres,mongodb,dynamodb}_adapter.go.tmpl, *_repository.go.tmpl, tests, database_module.go.tmpl
APIda/api/api_client.go.tmpl, api_module.go.tmpl
Cacheda/cache/cache_adapter.go.tmpl, cache_module.go.tmpl
Config snippetsconfig/{postgres,mongodb,dynamodb}.yaml.tmpl

Helpers​

Templates use functions such as toLower, toCamelCase registered on the engine. Keep new templates valid Go after execution so gofmt succeeds.
